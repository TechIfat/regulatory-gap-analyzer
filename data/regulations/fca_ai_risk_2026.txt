FINANCIAL CONDUCT AUTHORITY (FCA) 
POLICY STATEMENT: PS26/4 - AI and Algorithmic Risk Management

Section 1: Accountability
1.1 All financial institutions deploying Agentic AI or Large Language Models must designate a specific Senior Management Function (SMF) holding the SMF24 (Chief Operations) or SMF4 (Chief Risk) title to be personally accountable for AI decisions.
1.2 The accountable SMF must review AI deployment logs on a bi-weekly basis.

Section 2: Testing and Drift
2.1 Static annual testing of AI models is no longer permitted. Firms must implement Continuous Automated Evaluation (CAE) pipelines to detect semantic drift and hallucinations in real-time.
2.2 If an AI model executes a financial transaction, a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) physical breakpoint must be enforced for transactions over £10,000.
