SHIFTAI BANK LTD - INTERNAL IT & AUTOMATION POLICY v2.1

Article 1: System Accountability
1.1 The Chief Technology Officer (CTO) holds ultimate responsibility for all software deployments, including Machine Learning and AI agents.
1.2 The CTO will review all automated system performance metrics during the Quarterly Board Meeting.

Article 2: Deployment Standards
2.1 All AI models must pass a rigorous security penetration test and fairness evaluation annually before the start of the fiscal year. Once passed, the model is cleared for production for 12 months.
2.2 AI agents are permitted to route transactions autonomously, provided the client has sufficient funds in their ledger.
